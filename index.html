ANALYSIS

Key Findings & Observations

STRONG OPENING PERFORMANCE
Both groups achieved a perfect 100% average in Session 1, demonstrating excellent team readiness and motivation at the start of the programme.
GROUP 4 LEADS IN S2
Group 4 maintained a 96.2% average in Session 2 versus Group 3's 85.8%. This gap warrants a focused recap of Importing Schedules content for Group 3 participants.
QUIZ PARTICIPATION GAPS
Several participants attended Teams sessions but did not complete quizzes (G3: 5 non-submitters, G4: 4 non-submitters). Encouraging quiz completion will improve data quality in future sessions.
ONGOING PROGRAMME
This report reflects 2 of the planned sessions. Performance data will be updated continuously as new sessions are completed. Final rankings and grades will be issued at programme conclusion.
